Bug Killer — Crashed Anaconda
Another great way of collecting higher-end raw materials is to farm materials at a crashed site in HIP 16613 on planetary body 1 A named the "Bug Killer" where there exists a crashed Anaconda with Cargo Racks scattered around. You can find some high-grade Raw Materials by shooting and destroying these Cargo Racks.
HIP 16613 · Planet 1 A · (-11.0093, -95.6755)
Antimony G4 Ruthenium G4 Technetium G4 Tellurium G4 Tungsten G3
You might get paid a friendly visit by a Thargoid Interceptor, but do not worry, you are safe from being shot. The Interceptor is only there to survey the area.
  • Shoot and Collect the Raw Materials from these Cargo Racks
  • Menu Log to Reset the Materials
